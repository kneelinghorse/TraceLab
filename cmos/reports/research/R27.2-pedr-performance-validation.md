# R27.2: PEDR Performance Validation Study

**Mission:** R27.2 - PEDR Performance Validation Study  
**Status:** Completed  
**Research Date:** 2025-12-28  
**Corpus Size:** 12 documents  
**Query Set:** 12 queries (graded relevance)  

---

## Executive Summary

This study validates PEDR quality-aware ranking and governance filters on the R27.1 baseline corpus and query set. The benchmark uses offline TF-IDF + BM25 retrieval and applies `QualityScoringService` with synthetic mission metadata to measure quality boosts, governance filtering, and relationship enrichment. Results show the quality scoring multiplier aligns with the 2x claim for complete vs draft missions, while governance filtering reliably removed PII-flagged items. Ranking metrics (precision/recall/nDCG) decreased versus the hybrid baseline, indicating a trade-off that should be addressed in production tuning.

**Key outcomes:**
- **Quality boost validated:** complete missions average 1.25 vs draft 0.60 (2.08x).
- **Governance filtering accuracy:** 6 PII-flagged results in top-k, 6 removed (100% removal rate).
- **Relationship enrichment:** avg 18.33 related docs per query; 85% of results have at least one related item.
- **Ranking metrics:** PEDR quality-aware results underperform hybrid baseline on this synthetic metadata set, indicating tuning opportunities.

## 1. Methodology

### Corpus and Queries
- Corpus and queries from R27.1: `artifacts/benchmarks/benchmark-corpus/`.
- 12 queries with graded relevance judgments.

### Retrieval Baseline
- **Candidate retrieval:** TF-IDF + BM25 hybrid (semantic weight 0.7 / keyword weight 0.3).
- **Candidate pool:** top_k * 2 before quality scoring (matches hybrid oversampling).

### PEDR Quality-Aware Layer
- Quality scoring applied via `QualityScoringService` with synthetic metadata (`quality_metadata.json`) and governance filters.
- Governance filters: `min_quality_gates=3`, `allow_pii=False`.

### Statistical Testing
- Per-query paired sign test (two-sided) for precision@k, recall@k, nDCG@k.

---

## 6. Gaps and Optimization Recommendations

1. **Ranking quality drop vs baseline:** Quality-aware scoring demotes relevant items when their mission status or gates are incomplete. Consider recalibrating boosts or using a smaller multiplier for low-confidence metadata.
2. **Metadata sensitivity:** Synthetic metadata is driving ranking changes; run the same benchmark on production metadata (PostgreSQL + Qdrant) to validate realistic effects.
3. **Candidate pool tuning:** Experiment with larger candidate multipliers (3–4) to give PEDR re-ranking more headroom.
4. **Governance tuning:** If governance filtering removes relevant content, consider staged filtering (soft penalty vs hard drop) for edge cases.
