# R27.1: RAG/Hybrid Search Baseline Research

**Mission:** R27.1 - RAG/Hybrid Search Baseline Research
**Status:** Completed
**Research Date:** 2025-12-27
**Corpus Size:** 12 documents
**Query Set:** 12 queries (graded relevance)

---

## Executive Summary

This mission establishes a reproducible baseline for standard RAG and hybrid search retrieval so PEDR can be compared against consistent metrics. A curated corpus was built from existing TraceLab documentation, paired with a query set and graded relevance judgments. Baseline retrieval was evaluated using offline TF-IDF (semantic) and TF-IDF + BM25 fusion (hybrid) aligned to the hybrid search weighting in `app/services/hybrid_search.py`.

Key outcomes:
- **Hybrid baseline improves recall and nDCG** versus semantic-only retrieval on the same corpus and query set.
- **Latency is sub-millisecond** because the benchmark is offline and CPU-only; results are algorithmic baselines, not production timings.
- All artifacts and scripts are reproducible and versioned in the repository.

---

## 1. Benchmark Landscape (Survey)

To align with industry-standard evaluation approaches, the baseline design mirrors conventions from common retrieval benchmarks:
- **MTEB** and **BEIR** emphasize retrieval accuracy and ranking quality across heterogeneous corpora.
- Core metrics consistently reported include **precision@k**, **recall@k**, **nDCG@k**, and **latency**.

This baseline follows those conventions to make PEDR comparisons defensible and repeatable.

---

## 2. Baseline Definitions

### Standard RAG Baseline (Semantic)
- **Retrieval:** TF-IDF cosine similarity (semantic-only).
- **Top-K:** 5 results per query.
- **Purpose:** Deterministic proxy for embedding-based retrieval without requiring Qdrant or OpenAI dependencies.

### Standard Hybrid Baseline
- **Retrieval:** TF-IDF + BM25.
- **Fusion:** Min-max normalization per modality with weighted combination.
- **Weights:** 0.7 semantic / 0.3 keyword (aligned to `docs/hybrid-search.md`).
- **Top-K:** 5 results per query.

---

## 3. Corpus and Query Set

### Corpus
The benchmark corpus is a curated snapshot of TraceLab documentation and sprint research reports:
- Stored at `artifacts/benchmarks/benchmark-corpus/`.
- ASCII-sanitized text snapshots for deterministic tokenization.
- Manifest with source paths, hashes, and word counts in `corpus_manifest.json`.

### Query Set
- Stored at `artifacts/benchmarks/benchmark-corpus/queries.json`.
- 12 queries with graded relevance judgments (2 = primary, 1 = secondary).
- Covers PEDR search, hybrid fusion, Qdrant optimization, resilience, quality gates, and sprint outcomes.

---

## 4. Evaluation Methodology

1. **Fixed corpus and query set** across all methods.
2. **Top-K = 5** for every query to match standard retrieval reporting.
3. **Deterministic scoring** (TF-IDF and BM25) to avoid external dependencies.
4. **Metrics computed per query**, then averaged across the suite.
5. **Latency captured per query** (CPU-only, local runtime) to provide algorithmic baseline.

---

## 5. Results Summary

Metrics are captured in `artifacts/benchmarks/rag-baseline-metrics.json`.

| Method | Precision@5 | Recall@5 | nDCG@5 | P50 Latency (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic (TF-IDF) | 0.250 | 0.792 | 0.786 | 0.054 |
| Hybrid (TF-IDF + BM25) | 0.283 | 0.875 | 0.813 | 0.130 |

Interpretation:
- **Hybrid improves recall (+0.083) and nDCG (+0.027)** over semantic-only retrieval.
- Precision@5 remains low due to the small corpus and overlapping topical vocabulary.
